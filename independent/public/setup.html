Choose your voice provider

Gemini is the simple starting point. For a cloned voice, use an agent or configuration you have already created at ElevenLabs or Hume.

Gemini — start with a free voice

Create a key in Google AI Studio. Keep the Google project on the Free tier with billing off, then paste the key above and send it over USB. No Agent ID is needed.

Google’s Live API uses preset voices. Free access depends on your project’s current quota.

ElevenLabs — use your existing cloned-voice agent

In ElevenLabs Agents, choose an agent configured with your voice or clone, personality, and first greeting. Choose ElevenLabs above, paste its API key and Agent ID, and send them over USB.

The agent needs PCM 16 kHz input, PCM output, and agent_response_complete enabled in its client events. Cloning and conversations follow your ElevenLabs plan.

Hume — use your existing voice configuration

Choose a configuration in Hume with your desired voice and personality. Choose Hume above, paste its API key and Config ID, and send them over USB. Voice access and usage follow your Hume plan.

Your settings are remembered. Hold Fn + W for two seconds to change Wi-Fi, provider, or key later.

With Gemini, one minute without speech enters STANDBY. Press Enter to continue the conversation. Gemini can resume for up to two hours; restarting the Cardputer or an expired session starts fresh.

BYOK has no app-imposed daily voice-time or connection-count allowance. Your provider's quotas, plan, and charges still apply. The shared bridge supports two simultaneous conversations, with one active connection per key. Sessions briefly reconnect within four minutes to meet hosting limits. This bridge does not store visitor chats; Gemini resumption keeps its session handle in the Cardputer's memory.
